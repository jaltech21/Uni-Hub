# UNIMTECH Project Report Guideline Checklist

Source: **UMT - SOT - GUIDELINES FOR PROJECT REPORT.pdf**  
Institution: **University of Management and Technology (UNIMTECH)**  
School: **School of Technology**

This checklist records the requirements extracted during Phase 1. The current
report has not yet been compared against these requirements; that comparison
belongs to Phase 2.

## Required structure and order

The guide requires these sections in this order:

1. Title Page
2. Table of Contents
3. Project Overview
4. Project Execution / Development Methodology
5. Findings and Data Presentation / Product Evaluation and Outcomes
6. Discussion and Interpretation / Analysis and Reflective Discussion
7. Conclusion and Recommendation(s)
8. Appendices (optional)

For a development-based project, the guide uses the following preferred names:

- **Development Methodology**
- **Product Evaluation and Outcomes**
- **Analysis and Reflective Discussion**

## Section requirements

### 1. Title Page

Include:

- Approved project title
- Student name
- Student ID number
- Supervisor name
- University name
- Department
- Submission date

The title page should be simple and professional, following the guide's
example.

### 2. Table of Contents

Include the report sections in their final order with accurate page numbers.

### 3. Project Overview

Purpose: provide a short summary of the project and its goals.

Include:

- A short description of the project topic in 2–3 sentences
- The main objective or question addressed
- A brief statement of the approach used
- Length of **150–200 words**

### 4. Development Methodology

Purpose: explain how the product was built, step by step.

Include:

- Development phases
- Tools used and why each was selected
- Iterations, changes, and fixes
- Technical challenges and solutions
- Rationale for development choices
- Specific code or design details where useful
- Visuals such as screenshots or diagrams where helpful
- Length of **4–6 pages**

The guide describes this as the core of the report and asks for enough detail
that another person could understand or repeat the process.

### 5. Product Evaluation and Outcomes

Purpose: show what was created and how it performed.

Include:

- The final product
- Product screenshots or other relevant visuals
- Testing results
- User testing results, if performed
- Factual metrics such as speed, errors, or completion results when available
- Clear labels and references for visuals
- Length of **1–2 pages**

Keep this section factual. Interpretation belongs in Section 6.

### 6. Analysis and Reflective Discussion

Purpose: interpret the results and reflect on the development experience.

Include:

- What the results show
- Whether results matched expectations
- Surprises or unexpected findings
- Implications of the results
- What worked well
- What should be changed
- Lessons learned
- Future improvements
- Honest technical reflection tied to specific project activities
- Length of **3–5 pages**

The guide specifically encourages use of **“I”** so that the section reflects
the student's own experience.

### 7. Conclusion and Recommendation(s)

Include:

- A 1–2 sentence summary of what was achieved
- Key lessons learned
- Practical recommendations and future work
- Length of **0.5–1 page**

### 8. Appendices

Optional supporting material may include:

- Raw data
- Code
- Survey questions
- Extra visuals
- Other useful supporting evidence

Each appendix should be referenced from the main report where relevant. Do not
include unnecessary material.

## Development-project interpretation

The guide states that software, prototype, and app projects should focus on:

- How the product was developed
- Why methods and tools were chosen
- What iterations occurred
- What technical challenges were encountered
- What the final product produced
- What was learned from testing and reflection

The guide does not require a dissertation-style research structure. The report
should demonstrate practical work and critical thinking.
